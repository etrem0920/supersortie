    Pilot briefing

    How to fly

    Steer

    Point the mouse where you want to fly, or use A D / ← →. Guns fire automatically.

    Barrel roll

    Click or press Space. You burst forward, bullets pass through you, and homing missiles lose their lock.

    Chain kills

    Kills close together build a combo up to ×5. Beat each wave's par time without getting hit for big bonuses.
